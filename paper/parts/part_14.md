the joint program between the
Nuclear Science and Engineering department and the Center for
Computational Science and Engineering at MIT. He joined MIT
in September 2019 after receiving an Engineering Diploma and
a Master in Nuclear Engineering
from France. At MIT he received
a dual Master of Science from the
Nuclear Science and Engineering
(NSE) and the Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
(EECS) departments.
Koroush Shirvan is an associate professor of nuclear science
and engineering. He specializes in accelerating innovations
in nuclear energy to improve
the sustainability of current and
next generation power plants.
His approach combines multiple
scales, physics and disciplines to
realize innovative solutions in the
highly regulated nuclear energy
sector. Prof. Shirvan received his
PhD from MIT in 2012. He is also
the 2022 recipient of the Reactor
Technology Award given by the
American Nuclear Society.
